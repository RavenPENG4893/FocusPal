Features
Everything a companion should be
FocusPal combines system awareness, intelligent reminders, and playful interactions into a living desktop creature.
🖥

Scene Recognition

Knows if you're coding, writing, browsing, or in a meeting. Reacts differently to each — your companion truly understands your workflow.

🧠

Hybrid AI Brain

Local Qwen model works offline. Cloud DeepSeek powers deeper conversations. Your data never leaves your machine.

⏱

Focus Timer + ML Prediction

A focus timer that learns your patterns. ML prediction tells you when you're most likely to complete a session.

🎒

Clipboard Backpack

Copy something — your companion catches it as a glowing orb. Browse clipboard history from the pixel backpack.

🎣

Network Fishing

Download speed = fishing rod intensity. Downloading a big file? Watch your companion reel in a trophy fish.

🪟

Window Hide-and-Seek

The character dodges, hides, and peeks when windows approach. It exists in the same spatial reality as your apps.

🔔

Smart Reminders

Thompson Sampling learns when you actually follow reminders. Water, stretch, and eye breaks at the perfect time.

🏆

77 Collectibles

Discover items during autonomous adventures, unlock focus milestones, and fill your collection book.
